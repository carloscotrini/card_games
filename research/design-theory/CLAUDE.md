# research/design-theory/

Theory, frameworks, and papers on *how to design* good games — the conceptual toolkit
for inventing our own two-player standard-deck game. (Not a survey of existing games;
that lives in [`../existing-games/`](../existing-games/CLAUDE.md).)

## Contents

- [`principles.md`](principles.md) — organized across six threads (combinatorial game
  theory, depth-vs-complexity, designer wisdom, balance/fairness, meaningful decisions,
  academic evaluation) and ending in a **12-point design checklist** with sourced links.

## Headline ideas

- **Elegance = high depth / low complexity.** Generate depth from rule *interactions*,
  not rule count.
- **Define the victory condition first** (Knizia) — it dictates every meaningful decision.
- **Hidden information** is the primary engine of depth, bluffing, and yomi.
- **Neutralize first-player advantage** (pie/swap rule, opening bid, cut-and-choose).
- **Audit every turn for dominant strategies**; add intransitive (RPS) counters.
- **Evaluate with measurable metrics** (Browne/Ludi): low draw rate, frequent lead
  changes, decisive endings, forcing moves.
