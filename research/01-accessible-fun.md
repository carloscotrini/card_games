# Category 1 — Accessible & fun

Games that are extremely easy to teach (~1-minute rules), light, quick, and genuinely
fun for casual two-player sessions. Accessibility and fun come first; depth is secondary.

## Top picks

### 1. Speed (aka Spit, Slam)
- **2p fit:** Native, and the *best* format — real-time, head-to-head, no turns.
- **Rules:** Two center piles; both players simultaneously lay hand cards one rank above/below either center pile (A wraps to K), refilling from a draw pile. First to empty all cards wins.
- **Why it's fun:** Frantic, no downtime, instant rematches — pure flow.
- **Teach time:** ~1 min. **Deck:** one 52, no jokers.
- Source: https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/spit

### 2. Egyptian Ratscrew (ERS, "Slap")
- **2p fit:** Excellent — fewer hands means faster pile turnover.
- **Rules:** Players blindly flip cards to a center pile; face cards/aces force the opponent to "pay" (4/3/2/1 cards). Slap on doubles/sandwiches to steal the pile; win all cards.
- **Why it's fun:** Physical, loud, swingy; the slap and face-card duels create tension and laughter.
- **Teach time:** ~1–2 min. **Deck:** one 52.
- Source: https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/egyptian-rat-screw

### 3. Golf (6-card)
- **2p fit:** Great natively; tight and quick. (9-card needs two decks — use 6-card for 2p.)
- **Rules:** Each player gets a 2×3 mostly-face-down grid; draw from stock/discard to swap and lower your total. Matched columns score zero. Lowest total over 9 "holes" wins.
- **Why it's fun:** Easy push-your-luck calls, satisfying "zero a column" moments.
- **Teach time:** ~1–2 min. **Deck:** one 52.
- Source: https://www.pagat.com/draw/golf.html

### 4. GOPS / Goofspiel
- **2p fit:** Native, designed for 2 — a clean duel. (Also the elegant deep outlier; see [category 2](02-simple-but-deep.md).)
- **Rules:** Each player holds one full suit (A–K). Flip a card from the diamond "prize" suit; both secretly bid a card, higher bid wins the prize. Most points after 13 rounds wins.
- **Why it's fun:** Pure mind-game bluffing, zero luck once dealt; casual players love the "read your opponent" feel.
- **Teach time:** ~1 min. **Deck:** one 52 (three suits used).
- Source: https://www.pagat.com/misc/gops.html

## Strong additional options

- **Garbage / Trash** — Fill a 10-card A–10 layout; Jacks wild, Q/K dead. Light puzzle, great with kids. ~2 min, one 52. https://www.pagat.com/patience/trash.html
- **Ninety-Nine** — Play cards onto a running total without exceeding 99; special card values; can't play = lose a token. (At 2p, a 4 grants another turn.) Quick mental tension. ~1 min, one 52. https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/99-ninety-nine/
- **Scopa (lite)** — Capture table cards by matching value/sum; sweeps bonus; race to 11. Native and excellent at 2p; for casual play just score "most cards." ~2–3 min, 40-card deck (52 minus 8/9/10). https://www.pagat.com/fishing/scopa.html
- **Slapjack** — Flip to a center pile, first to slap each Jack takes it; collect all 52. A gentler ERS. ~30 sec, one 52. https://bicyclecards.com/how-to-play/slapjack
- **Snap** — Flip cards, shout "Snap!" on a match to win piles. Thinner than Slapjack at 2p. ~30 sec, one 52. https://www.pagat.com/war/snap.html
- **Nertz / Pounce** — Real-time speed-solitaire race onto shared ace foundations. *Flag:* 2p is slower / prone to stalling, and needs **two decks** (different backs). Shines with more players. ~3–4 min. https://www.pagat.com/patience/nerts.html

## Famous but weaker at 2p (honest flags)

- **War** — Native 2p but mindless and long; zero decisions. Young kids only. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_(card_game)
- **Beggar-My-Neighbour** — Pure luck, no decisions, can run nearly indefinitely. Charming idea, tedious in practice. https://www.pagat.com/war/beggar_my_neighbour.html

## Synthesis

For a casual two-player session, **Speed/Spit** is the strongest single pick:
1-minute teach, one deck, zero downtime, immediate "again!" energy. Pair it with
**Egyptian Ratscrew** for loud physical chaos and **Golf (6-card)** for something
calmer with light decisions. For cerebral pairs, **GOPS** is the elegant outlier —
no luck, all bluff, still explainable in a minute. Avoid War and Beggar-My-Neighbour
except with young children.
