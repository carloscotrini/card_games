# research/existing-games/

Surveys of real two-player card games, split into three categories by the
accessibility/depth/complexity trade-off. Each file is self-contained: per-game entries
(2p fit, rules, source of depth, learning curve, deck) plus a synthesis.

## Contents

- [`01-accessible-fun.md`](01-accessible-fun.md) — **Accessible & fun.** ~1-minute teach,
  light, fun-first; depth secondary. Top picks: Speed/Spit, Egyptian Ratscrew, Golf, GOPS.
- [`02-simple-but-deep.md`](02-simple-but-deep.md) — **Simple but deep** (the core
  category). Minimal rules, large strategic depth. Elite tier: Goofspiel/GOPS, Schnapsen,
  German Whist, Cribbage.
- [`03-complex-strategic.md`](03-complex-strategic.md) — **Complex & strategic.**
  Heavyweight rules with deep strategy. Best payoff: Piquet, Klaberjass.

## Notes

- Categories overlap at the edges (Schnapsen and Piquet appear in both 02 and 03); each
  file notes the cross-reference.
- Honest flags are kept in-file: famous-but-shallow games (War, Beggar-My-Neighbour) and
  complexity-without-payoff games (Two-Handed Canasta, Honeymoon Bridge) are called out.
