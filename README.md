# card_games

Investigating simple-yet-deeply-strategic card games playable with a **standard
52-card deck**, focused on **two-player variants**. The end goal is to design our
own elegant two-player card game.

## Research

Initial landscape research lives in [`research/`](research/), organized into four
categories:

1. [Accessible & fun](research/01-accessible-fun.md) — quick to teach, light, genuinely fun.
2. [Simple but deep](research/02-simple-but-deep.md) — "easy to learn, hard to master"; the core interest.
3. [Complex & strategic](research/03-complex-strategic.md) — heavyweight rules with deep strategy.
4. [Design principles](research/04-design-principles.md) — theory and frameworks to inform our own design.

See [`research/README.md`](research/README.md) for the cross-cutting summary and
shortlist of candidates worth playtesting.

### Scope constraints

- Standard 52-card deck (stripped/doubled variants noted explicitly; jokers only where flagged).
- Two players.
- No proprietary or special decks.
